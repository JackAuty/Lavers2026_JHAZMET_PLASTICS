Title: Ingested plastic drives physiological variation independent of body mass in seabird fledglings
Journal: Journal of Hazardous Materials: Plastic
Manuscript ID: HAZMP-D-26-00137
Authors: Jennifer L. Lavers, Alix M. de Jersey, Jack Rivers-Auty, Alexander L. Bond
Corresponding author: Dr Jennifer L. Lavers, jennifer.lavers1@nhm.ac.uk; Statistic analyses question to Jack Auty jackrauty@gmail.com


Overview

This repository contains the R code used to reproduce the statistical analyses and figures for the manuscript. The study examines relationships between ingested plastic mass, body mass, and blood chemistry in fledgling Sable Shearwaters (Ardenna carneipes), including comparisons between historical and contemporary sampling periods and mediation analyses testing body mass as a potential mediator.
